The Story of Eventindia

A small studio in with one big belief — every celebration deserves to feel effortless and unforgettable.

Our Journey

From One Hall to Hundreds of Stories

Eventindia Events began in 2014 in , Agra, with a single decorated banquet and a stubborn obsession with detail. What started as a two-person crew is today a 45+ member team trusted by families, founders and brands across Agra and Delhi NCR.

We've always believed events aren't about flower walls or light rigs alone — they're about how people feel when they walk in. That belief shapes every floor plan, every playlist, every carefully timed entrance we create.

  • ✓ 500+ events delivered across Uttar Pradesh
  • ✓ In-house design, production & hospitality teams
  • ✓ COVID-era virtual & hybrid event specialists

Chapter One · 2014

It started with one wedding.

A 400-guest reception in a banquet hall. Two people. One borrowed van. And a promise that every guest would remember the night.

Chapter Two · 2017

We built our own atelier.

Floral fabrication, drapes, lighting — all in-house. Costs fell, creative control rose, and clients noticed the difference within a single event.

Chapter Three · 2020

We made celebrations pandemic-proof.

Hybrid weddings, sanitised hospitality, live streaming — 42 events delivered during the hardest year the industry has ever seen.

Chapter Four · 2023

A dedicated corporate wing.

Conferences, product launches, award nights — a B2B vertical trusted by brands across Agra and Delhi NCR.

Chapter Five · Today

500 events. Still counting.

A 45-member crew, 200+ vendor partners, and a client roster that keeps coming back. The story isn't finished — it's just getting good.
